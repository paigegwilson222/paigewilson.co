The ugly phase is welcome here.

Each month, Lori and I start with the same materials and make two different things. Try either idea, borrow a little from both, or follow a curiosity of your own. We’ll bring the supplies. You can come without a plan.

You know that moment when the thing in your hands looks nothing like the thing you pictured? We make room for that here. Try the weird idea. Change your mind. Leave it unfinished. The part you almost covered up might turn out to be your favorite.

This is an evening for adults who miss making things just to see what happens. No art experience needed.
